SOMA Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

SOMA is a study planner for medical students on iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what SOMA does with your information. In short: your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. The developer never receives it.

What SOMA stores

Your courses, subjects, lectures, exams, study steps, notes, drawings, PDF annotations, focus sessions and XP are stored on your device. If you turn on Sync with iCloud, they are also stored in your private iCloud database, which Apple operates and only you can access. SOMA has no servers and no accounts, and it does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Files you import

Programs (Excel, CSV or text files) and slides you choose are read on your device. Copies of slides are kept in SOMA's own storage so they work offline.

Your device's calendars

With your permission, SOMA reads the calendars on your device to show your classes next to your study plan and to keep linked lectures at their current times. SOMA does not change your calendars.

Google Calendar and Google Drive

Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, SOMA asks for read-only access:

  • calendar.readonly to show your Google Calendar events in SOMA and keep lectures linked to them up to date.
  • drive.readonly to let you browse a folder you choose and download the slides (PDFs and Google Slides, exported as PDF) that you attach to lectures.

Data from Google travels directly between your device and Google. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else, it is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Downloaded slides are stored on your device. Links to the Drive files you attach are saved with your lectures, in your private iCloud if sync is on. SOMA keeps your Google sign-in token in your iCloud Keychain.

SOMA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can sign out at any time in SOMA › Settings › Google, which also revokes SOMA's access. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Study steps written on your device

When SOMA writes study steps with Apple Intelligence, the lecture title and slide text are processed on your device by Apple's on-device model and are not sent anywhere.

Videos

YouTube videos you add play in YouTube's embedded player (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube's own privacy policy applies to what you watch there.

Deleting your data

SOMA › Settings › Erase all data deletes your courses, notes, progress and slide copies, including the copy in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on that device.
